# College Academic Assistant - Data Instructions

This folder contains the college documents used by the AI-Based College Academic Assistant.

Add college-related information to the following text files:

1. 3rd_year_cse_syllabus.txt

   * Third-year CSE syllabus
   * Semester-wise subjects
   * Subject names and course details

2. academic_regulations.txt

   * Academic rules and regulations
   * Attendance requirements
   * Credit requirements
   * Promotion criteria

3. examination_guidelines.txt

   * Internal assessment rules
   * Semester examination guidelines
   * Passing criteria
   * Revaluation and supplementary examination rules

4. internship_guidelines.txt

   * Internship eligibility
   * Application procedure
   * Internship duration
   * Internship report and evaluation requirements

5. placement.txt

   * Placement eligibility criteria
   * Placement process
   * Companies visiting the college
   * Placement statistics and packages

6. cse_subjects.txt

   * Third-year CSE subject details
   * Subject descriptions
   * Important topics
   * Credits and practical components

7. college_faq.txt

   * Frequently asked student questions
   * Answers to academic queries
   * Important academic contacts or office details, if appropriate

8. study_resources.txt

   * Recommended textbooks
   * Reference materials
   * Learning resources
   * Subject-wise preparation tips

INSTRUCTIONS:

* Enter verified college information in the appropriate files.
* Use clear headings and paragraphs.
* Do not add invented college rules or statistics.
* You may add more .txt files if required.
* Save all files in UTF-8 text format.
* Open the Streamlit application and click "Build / Rebuild Knowledge Base" after adding or updating documents.

The AI assistant uses these documents to retrieve relevant information and answer college-specific questions.
