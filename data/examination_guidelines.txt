1. Attendance requirement
A student normally needs at least 85% attendance in each subject.
Up to 10% attendance may be condoned by the Principal for specified reasons such as medical grounds, university-level sports, cultural activities, seminars, workshops, or paper presentations.
If attendance falls below 75% in a course, the student is not permitted to appear for the SEE for that course and receives an 'N' grade.
The student must then re-register for the course when it is offered again.
Attendance for both CIE and SEE is compulsory.
2. CIE — Continuous Internal Evaluation

CIE is the internal assessment conducted during the semester.

It can include activities such as:

Internal tests/Mid-Semester Examinations
Quizzes
Assignments
Tutorials
Laboratory work
Problem solving
Projects
Seminars
Other course-specific activities

The exact CIE components and weightage depend on the applicable course/regulation. The 2023 CSE curriculum uses CIE and SEE components for course evaluation.

3. SEE — Semester End Examination

SEE is the university/semester-end examination covering the prescribed course syllabus.

For the 2023 CSE curriculum, the examination scheme is specified course-wise in the curriculum. Students must satisfy the eligibility requirements before appearing.

4. Examination application

Students have to complete the examination application process within the notified period.

NITTE's examination procedure states that examination application forms are hosted before the examination, with the exact period depending on the programme/scheme.

5. Hall ticket and ID card

Students appearing for theory examinations should carry:

Admission/Hall Ticket
College Identity Card

NITTE's published guidelines state that candidates are required to possess the admission card and identity card during theory examinations.

6. Examination hall rules

Students should:

Enter the examination hall with the required documents.
Follow the assigned seating arrangement.
Maintain strict silence.
Follow instructions given by the invigilator.
Write the examination using the prescribed answer booklet.
Not detach portions of the answer booklet.
Not use prohibited materials or engage in communication with other candidates.

NITTE's examination procedure specifically requires strict silence and controlled entry into the examination hall.

7. Malpractice

Malpractice during an examination can result in the student being booked for malpractice.

According to NITTE's published procedure, a candidate found indulging in malpractice may be removed from the examination hall, and the answer booklet and admission card may be seized and submitted as a malpractice case.

8. Absence from examination

For NMAMIT's 2023 B.Tech regulations:

Attendance in all CIE and SEE examinations of each registered course is compulsory, with no provision for re-examinations.

This means your academic assistant should not tell a student that they can automatically rewrite a missed CIE/SEE. The student should instead be directed to the applicable college/university examination authority for their particular situation.

9. Results and marks

NITTE publishes examination-related information through its examination section, including:

Examination procedures

Results
Attendance and marks

Timetables
10. Retotalling and answer-script viewing

NITTE's published examination procedure states that students can apply for retotalling within 10 days of declaration of results, subject to the prescribed fee.
